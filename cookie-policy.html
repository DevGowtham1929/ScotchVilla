Cookie Policy

Here’s how optional analytics and your cookie choice work on Scotchvilla.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites may store in your browser. Scotchvilla uses a local browser preference to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics, so we don’t ask you again each time you visit.

Google Analytics

If enabled, Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use Scotchvilla. Analytics is optional and will only be activated after you choose “Accept Analytics.” If you choose “Reject Analytics,” Google Analytics will not be loaded. You can still use the website either way.

Your choice

Your choice is saved in this browser. You can update it at any time below. You can also clear this site’s local storage in your browser settings to be asked again.
