A LITTLE COOKIE CLARITY

Cookie Policy

Here’s how optional analytics and your cookie choice work on Scotchvilla.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites may store in your browser. Scotchvilla uses a local browser preference to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics, so we don’t ask you again each time you visit.

Google Analytics

If enabled, Google Analytics helps us understand which pages people visit and the browsers, devices and approximate regions they use. Analytics is optional and only loads after you choose “Accept Analytics.” If you choose “Reject Analytics,” the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. You can use the website either way.

When you accept, this site sends analytics events to Scotchvilla’s Google Analytics property. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish a browser and report site usage. Google’s Analytics data and privacy information explains the data it collects and how it handles IP addresses.

Your choice

Scotchvilla saves only your accept or reject choice in this browser’s local storage. The site does not save visitor analytics in a Scotchvilla database; accepted analytics data is reported in Google Analytics. You can update your choice at any time below, or clear this site’s local storage in your browser settings to be asked again.
