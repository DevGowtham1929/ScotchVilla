Privacy Policy

How Scotchvilla protects your data, handles enquiries, and respects your privacy.

Scotchvilla respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, who we share it with and what choices you have.

If you are under 18, please read this policy with a parent or guardian.

1. Who We Are

Scotchvilla is a character-led brand inspired by Scotch, a dwarf Beagle. We are based in India.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@scotchvilla.com.

Our website is developed using Visual Studio Code (VS Code), HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript (ES6+) and hosted by Hostinger.

The website does not offer customer accounts, online checkout or its own customer database. We use selected third-party services to manage enquiries and understand website activity.

2. What Information We Collect

When you contact us:

  • WhatsApp: When you click our contact button and send a message, we receive your message and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Contact Form: We collect the information you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number and message.
  • Email: When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you share.
  • Instagram: When you message us, we receive your Instagram username and message.

When you enquire about merchandise:

Products cannot be purchased directly through this website. If you contact us to make a purchase, we may collect the details needed to complete your order, such as your name, contact information, delivery address and order details.

We do not collect payment card details through this website.

When you browse our website:

Our hosting and analytics services may collect technical information, such as your browser type, device information, pages visited and general website activity.

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand website usage, subject to your analytics cookie preferences.

3. Why We Collect Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries, messages and feedback.
  • Handle merchandise orders and related communication.
  • Respond to press, event and collaboration requests.
  • Improve our website and visitor experience through analytics, where permitted.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We only use personal information for relevant and lawful purposes.

4. Consent and Your Choices

When you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry or request.

Where your consent is required, including for optional analytics cookies, we ask for it separately.

You can withdraw your consent or request that we stop using your information by emailing hello@scotchvilla.com.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was already lawful or information we must retain under applicable law.

5. Who We Share Information With

We share information only when necessary to operate our website, communicate with you or fulfil your requests.

These services may include:

  • Scotchvilla Team: Authorised team members handling messages, enquiries and orders.
  • Web3Forms: Processes and forwards submissions from our website contact form.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram (Meta): Handle communications made through their platforms.
  • Google Analytics 4: Helps us understand website activity, subject to your analytics preferences.
  • Hostinger: Provides website hosting and related technical services.
  • Order and Delivery Providers: Where necessary to complete a merchandise purchase.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Third-Party and Embedded Content

Our website may include content from platforms such as Instagram and YouTube.

When you interact with embedded content, those platforms may collect information or use cookies according to their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing their privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage orders, maintain relevant records or comply with applicable law.

Contact messages and order-related records may be retained where needed for ongoing communication, customer support or legal requirements.

Information processed by third-party services, including Web3Forms, WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Analytics 4, may also be subject to their retention settings and policies.

Google Analytics 4 applies its configured data-retention settings to relevant user-level and event-level data.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete it or ensure it is no longer personally identifiable, where appropriate.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request that we:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Delete personal information that is no longer required.
  • Stop using information where you withdraw consent.

To make a request, email hello@scotchvilla.com.

We aim to respond within 30 days or within the period required by applicable law.

We will take reasonable steps to address your request for information under our control. Information held independently by third-party platforms may be subject to their own procedures.

You may also raise a privacy-related complaint with us.

9. Children's Privacy

Scotchvilla is a family-friendly brand, and visitors of all ages may browse our website.

However, enquiries, personal information submissions and merchandise purchases should be made by individuals aged 18 or above.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement and consent where required by law.

10. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality and understand visitor activity.

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website.

Optional analytics cookies are used according to your cookie consent choices.

You can manage your preferences through our cookie consent controls, where available, and your browser settings.

For more information, please read our separate Cookie Policy.

11. Keeping Your Information Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information shared with us.

These measures include secure website connections, appropriate access restrictions and the use of established third-party service providers.

However, no internet-based service is completely secure. We encourage you to share only the information necessary for your enquiry or purchase.

12. Grievance Officer

For privacy concerns, complaints or requests relating to your personal information, you may contact:

Akash

Brand & Business Head

Grievance Officer, Scotchvilla

Email: hello@scotchvilla.com

We will review your concern and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

For general privacy questions, you may also use the same email address.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website, services or applicable laws.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.

Contact: hello@scotchvilla.com © 2026 Scotchvilla. All rights reserved.
