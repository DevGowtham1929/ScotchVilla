Every great
world begins

It began with Scotch, a dwarf Beagle with an old soul, a gentle waddle and a deep devotion to coconuts. Today, it’s a little world where different is beautiful.

Meet Scotch

“Unhurried. Unbothered.
Unmistakably Scotch.”

Most Beagles chase the world. Scotch prefers to let it come to him. He takes life at the pace of a waddle, cuddles rather than barks and approaches every meal with complete devotion. He looks like a soft toy and behaves like an old soul. Somehow, that’s exactly what makes him unforgettable.

Every picture of me tells a small story. An ear flopped over a cushion. A nose pointed hopefully at the kitchen. A waddle, mid-adventure. (The adventure was snacks.) I never planned to be famous. I simply lay down, tilted my head and let the world fall in love. My family has been chasing me with a camera ever since. Not that I’m hard to catch. You’re welcome ;)

The Heart of Scotchvilla

Life, according to
One small Beagle.

Sometimes, we can be Scotch to someone else. Everything Scotchvilla makes, shares and dreams up comes back to four simple beliefs, learnt from one small dog who never needed to be anyone but himself.

Compassion

Scotch taught us that love means showing up for the hard days too. Quiet, consistent care beats grand gestures, every time.

Kindness

Kindness is cool. Not weak, not preachy, but joyful and confident. The gentlest dog we know proves it every single day.

Empathy & Inclusivity

Understanding someone’s world changes how we treat them. So there will always be room here for people, animals and personalities that don’t fit the mold.

Joy

A little happiness grows bigger when it’s passed on. Scotch started by cheering up strangers online. We intend to keep going.

Forever pup!

Dwarfism Made Me a
Little Different, but
It Never Made Me Less!

I was around seven months old when DNA tests and X-rays finally gave us an answer. It explained my small size and my little waddle. It changed nothing about how much I’m loved.

What it is

Dwarfism in dogs is a structural skeletal condition, not just a cute look. It shapes how a dog grows and can bring other health complications too.

How we found out

Akash’s colleague first noticed I wasn’t growing. After diet changes and several vets, a senior vet in Bengaluru recommended the tests that confirmed it.

How I’m cared for

I also manage hypothyroidism and an enlarged heart, so my diet, daily activity and regular vet visits get a little extra attention and care.

What it taught us

Smaller stature, different walk, same right to a full and happy life. When I was diagnosed, the vet said I’d be a blessing. Akash says he was right.
