WELCOME TO SCOTCHVILLA

Every great
world begins
small.

It all began with me — Scotch, a dwarf Beagle with an old soul, a gentle waddle and a deep devotion to coconuts. Today, I am the little dog behind Scotchvilla, a world where being different is beautiful.

Meet Scotch

“Unhurried. Unbothered.
Unmistakably me.”

Most Beagles chase the world. I prefer to let it come to me. I take life at a waddle’s pace, choose cuddles over barking and approach every meal with absolute devotion. I may look like a soft toy, but behave like an old soul. And somehow, that’s what makes me, well, me.

Every picture of mine tells a small story. An ear flopped over a cushion. A hopeful nose pointed towards the kitchen. A little waddle on a very important mission. (The mission being food). I never planned to be famous. I simply lay down, tilted my head and let the world fall in love. My family has been chasing me with a camera ever since. Not that I am hard to catch. You are welcome ;)

The Heart of Scotchvilla

Life, according to
One small Beagle.

You know what, hoomans? Sometimes, you can be someone else’s Scotch, too. Everything we make, share and dream up at Scotchvilla comes back to four little beliefs I have instilled in my hoomans, simply by being myself.

Compassion

I have shown my hoomans that love means showing up, especially on hard days. Sometimes, a little extra care and a cuddle can mean the world.

Kindness

I have taught my hoomans that kindness is cool. It can be joyful, confident and a little cheeky. You don’t have to be big or loud to make a difference.

Empathy & Inclusivity

I may see the world a little differently, but that’s what makes me, me. I have helped my hoomans make room for those who look, live or experience life a little differently.

Joy

Sometimes, all it takes is a head tilt to make someone smile. I have been spreading little moments of joy, and we as a family hope to spread many more.

Forever pup!

Dwarfism Made Me a
Little Different, but
It Never Made Me Less!

I was around seven months old when DNA tests and X-rays finally gave us an answer. It explained my small size and my little waddle. It changed nothing about how much I’m loved.

What it is

Veterinary experts explain that dwarfism in dogs is more than just a cute, little appearance. It affects skeletal growth and can come with other health complications, too.

How my hoomans found out

Dad’s colleague first noticed I wasn’t growing. After diet changes and visits to several vets, a senior vet in Bengaluru finally recommended the tests that confirmed it.

How I am cared for

My hoomans keep a close eye on my diet, activity and health, with regular vet check-ups and extra care to manage my hypothyroidism and enlarged heart.

What it taught my hoomans

Smaller stature, different walk, same right to a full and happy life. When I was diagnosed, the vet said “I would be a blessing.” Dad says he was right.
